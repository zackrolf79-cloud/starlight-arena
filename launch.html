3D or Simple 2D?

3D arena has swooping cameras and an in-ring camera. It needs a reasonably modern tablet or computer.
Simple 2D is lighter and runs on almost anything. If 3D runs slowly, the game switches to it by itself and remembers the choice.

Tip: on a phone, turn it sideways for the biggest arena.
